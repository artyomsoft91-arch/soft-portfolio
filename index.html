ABOUT

Hi!

I'm a video editor specializing in long-form YouTube content, including podcasts, interviews, and talking-head videos.

I take care of the structure, pacing, sound, graphics, and overall presentation. I remove anything that distracts from the message, highlight key moments, and use motion design only where it adds value.

To me, editing should never compete with the creator. Its role is to support the content and help communicate the message clearly.

I handle every project from the first cut to the final export and stay in touch throughout every stage.
